# Repository instructions

Follow the research and notebook conventions in `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Before proposing or launching experiments, read `notebooks/latent_space/experiment_results_index.md` and the relevant logs. Preserve exact recipes, splits, seeds, code hashes, job identities, failures, and source-wise metrics with valid/total counts. Update the index after collecting results. For notebook 07b, also update `notebooks/latent_space/07b_experiment_log.md`.

Prefer broad, hypothesis-driven matrices and multiple seeds when they resolve an open question. Reuse existing evidence and avoid needless duplication. Treat latent size as an empirical question: matched 2D/4D and, for noisy-LJ, 6D/8D comparisons. Do not introduce expert observable supervision or selection. Keep LJ graph-distance-two/three augmentation consistent in all stages.

The primary scientific success metric is post-fit p-ratio R², computed from reconstructed or autonomously predicted positions. Train on positions, trajectories, and graph structure only; keep p-ratio/strain out of inputs, losses, and checkpoint selection. Coordinate MSE and local-deformation diagnostics are secondary: higher position error can be acceptable with a compact latent if p-ratio R² is good. Never declare AE or rollout failure from coordinate MSE alone. Report AE reconstruction p-ratio R² and autonomous rollout p-ratio R² separately, source-wise and at stated horizons, with seed mean/SD and valid/total counts. If a recent study omitted this metric, evaluate its frozen checkpoints before deciding whether new training is needed; recover or rerun only missing artifacts. Preserve the estimator definition, physical-coordinate inversion, exact evaluation split, and final-test boundary.

Use `mendels_q`, `place=free:shared`, no host pins, and measured CPU/memory requests. Ordinary attention-AE runs default to 8 CPUs and 16 GB. Do not reserve whole nodes exclusively.

Engineering must optimize using ML only. Freeze the model, objective, constraints, budget, and final-design selection rule before optimization. Do not call LAMMPS to score proposals, accept/reject edits, stop optimization, or select restarts; do not initialize from a simulator-selected engineered design. Save and hash the final designs before final LAMMPS verification. Report every predeclared final design, including failures, without using verification to revise or select a winner. Historical simulator-in-the-loop examples do not establish ML-only engineering reliability. Keep any downstream response-calibrated latent readout explicitly identified; it is not an autonomous prediction of positions. Reliability requires held-out engineering results and cannot be inferred from reconstruction R² alone.

Keep notebook Markdown minimal, plots inline, and use the Editorial theme. Do not save figure files unless requested. Implement requested features directly rather than introducing optional switches without a reason.

The engineering goal is reliable gradient-guided modification of an original network using the frozen learned model only, including negative-p-ratio designs. Differentiate the downstream objective to spring stiffnesses and/or initial positions. Prefer the AE alone when sufficient; use the learned propagator if future-response prediction is needed. Follow `docs/research/engineering_goal.md`; an AE's reconstruction performance does not guarantee useful intervention gradients.

Delegate bounded operational tasks to GPT-5.6 Terra, medium (`gpt-5.6-terra`, `medium`) while the primary continues independent work. Terra may inspect configurations, submit and monitor PBS jobs, analyze logs/results, and make small experiment-specific changes. It must not redesign the architecture or change scientific objectives. Supply exact scope, owned files, relevant prior evidence, expected outputs, and stopping criteria. Use explicit model/effort overrides with limited or no history when supported. Reuse existing agents and assign one writer to shared logs.

Use `src/lss/dynamics/` and `notebooks/dynamics/` for AE/propagator work, and `src/lss/engineering/` and `notebooks/engineering/` for engineering. Retired implementations belong in `past_experiments`. New workflow APIs require concrete inputs and fail directly on missing inputs; avoid `None` fallbacks, placeholder results, and catch-all recovery. Preserve mathematical constraints and frozen historical snapshots. See `docs/project_structure.md`.
