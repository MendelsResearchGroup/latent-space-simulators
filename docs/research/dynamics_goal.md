# Shared latent-space simulator goal

Build one shared learned simulator that predicts network trajectories from the initial graph across Reid, low-T dePablo and noisy LJ, and transfers to mixed-temperature dePablo without training on mixed-T. The immediate research priority is noisy-LJ propagation.

The existing 2D message-passing autoencoder is the accepted reconstruction reference for this phase. Freeze its encoder, decoder and normalization statistics. Improve the propagator and its use of reference-graph information, temporal state or predictive distributions. A propagator may have a larger internal state while still predicting the 2D latent consumed by the frozen decoder. Poor autonomous prediction does not by itself justify restarting AE research.

Success is post-fit physical p-ratio R² from predicted positions, with step 100 as the primary horizon. Aim for noisy-LJ R² of at least 0.4 and close the gap to the matched AE reconstruction reference, while preserving Reid and low-T performance. Report the curve through steps 1–100, including early steps 2, 3 and 5; success does not require extending to step 199 first. The 0.4 target is a reporting goal, never a training loss or checkpoint-selection criterion.

The shared MP2D reconstruction reference at step 100 is Reid 0.957 ± 0.010, low-T dePablo 0.992 ± 0.00018 and noisy LJ 0.594 ± 0.064, across seeds 123/456/786. Each source has 20/20 valid validation networks per seed, or 60/60 repeated evaluations over 20 unique networks. These scores establish a useful working reference; they do not claim perfect LJ reconstruction or a mathematical ceiling on prediction. Exact matched values are in [the baseline tables](../../notebooks/results/lj100_propagator/baseline_summary.csv).

Train on positions, trajectories and graph structure only. Keep p-ratio and strain out of learned inputs, losses and checkpoint selection. Select checkpoints using the predeclared position/state validation criterion. For initial-frame-only experiments, supply the initial graph and latent only; generated history, internal memory and known step indices are permitted. In a separately declared observed-prefix experiment, the user has also authorized conditioning on frames 0, 1 and 2 to test whether real early motion resolves the prediction gap. No observed frame beyond the declared prefix may enter inference. Score only forecast frames and label this setting separately from initial-frame-only prediction; absolute step 100 is 98 predicted steps after frame 2. Preserve LJ graph-distance-two/three augmentation throughout.

Mixed-T is post-fit transfer evaluation only: no fitting, normalization estimation, checkpoint selection or matched low-T trajectory supplied at inference. For its thermal response, report the negative ratio of two strain-versus-frame OLS slopes across the trajectory prefix, alongside the separately labeled endpoint estimator. Preserve original reference-side groups and physical-coordinate inversion.

Report every declared approach, including failures and negative R², with source-wise seed mean/SD and valid/total counts. Separate observed-frame AE reconstruction, recursive autonomous prediction, direct trajectory prediction and generative ensemble means. Never choose a generative sample using its agreement with ground truth. Keep the shared study's final-test boundary intact; validation development is not proof of final-test or engineering reliability.

Use completed experiments to guide the next hypothesis, and distinguish architecture changes from changes in initialization or training budget. Record recipes, seeds, splits, hashes, job identities and outcomes in the [results index](../../notebooks/latent_space/experiment_results_index.md). Existing runs and historical evidence remain preserved.
